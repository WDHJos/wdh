About the Publications

Many publications in this database concern differences in happiness between individuals and groups, such as differences by age, gender, personality, financial situation, or social circumstances.

Other publications address differences in average happiness and the distribution of happiness across nations. Information on happiness in nations can also be found in the State of Nations database.

Purpose

The purpose of this bibliography remains unchanged: to understand how people can live happier lives, we need scientific knowledge about the conditions that foster happiness. Policy makers need information about the types of societies and organizations in which people thrive. Individuals need information about ways of life that contribute to happiness.

Each publication has been reviewed to determine whether it deals with happiness as the subjective appreciation of life as a whole.

Coverage

  • Approximately 18,000 publications
  • Near-complete coverage of social-science literature up to 2021
  • Publications in English, German, Dutch and other languages
  • Journal articles and dissertations
  • Links to full texts where available
