About the Bibliography

The bibliography originates from the World Database of Happiness, developed at Erasmus University Rotterdam.

The original database was created to collect and organize scientific publications concerning happiness as the subjective enjoyment of life as a whole.

The scope of the present website is to continue this bibliographic work and to provide researchers, students, policy makers and interested citizens with easy access to the scientific literature on happiness. It involves any scientific publication that refers to the subjective appreciation of life as a whole, even if this subject is only a side issue. Priority is given to publications with empirical results.

Not included are related fields such as mental health, social adjustment, alienation, and satisfaction with domains of life.
