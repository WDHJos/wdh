World Database of Happiness – Publications

Welcome to the World Database of Happiness – Publications.

This website continues the Bibliography section of the original World Database of Happiness (WDH), founded by Professor Ruut Veenhoven. The original WDH contains scientific knowledge on happiness as the subjective enjoyment of life as a whole and includes bibliographic information, research findings, studies, and happiness measures.

Not included are related fields such as mental health, social adjustment, alienation, and satisfaction with domains of life.

The original World Database of Happiness is no longer being expanded and has been frozen as a historical archive. However, it remains available online for consultation.

The present website continues and expands the Bibliography section of the World Database of Happiness, presented as an Excel list. It contains the complete bibliography of the original WDH and is continuously supplemented with new scientific publications, particularly publications published after 2020.

Users interested in studies, findings, and happiness measures are referred to the original World Database of Happiness.
